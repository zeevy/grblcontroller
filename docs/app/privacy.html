Grbl Controller Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

About this policy

This policy covers the Android app Grbl Controller by Silicon Circuits (package com.siliconcircuits.grblcontroller). It says what the app keeps, what it sends and why.

No account

The app has no account and no login. We run no server for the app. We do not sell your data.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, job history, saved WiFi boards, console history and console log files are kept only on your phone. They are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. The app reads only the G-code files you pick, and never uploads them.

Crash reports

When the app crashes, it sends a crash report through Google Firebase Crashlytics. A report has the phone model, the Android version, the app version, where in the code the crash happened, a random ID made by the app, the machine state (such as Idle or Run), the connection type and whether a job was running. It does not have your name, file names, G-code, device names or network addresses. Crash reports help us fix bugs.

Usage stats (off unless you turn them on)

Usage stats are off by default. You can turn them on in the app's setup guide, or in Settings > Application > Share Anonymous Usage Stats. When they are on, the app sends these through Google Firebase Analytics and Performance Monitoring:

  • the screens you use
  • for each job: its result, times, line counts, error codes, the firmware and the connection type

The app never sends file names, G-code, device names, network addresses or the console log.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth and WiFi: to connect to your machine.
  • Notifications: to show a running job and its result.
  • Run in the background: so a long job is not stopped when the phone closes apps.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Backups

If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may save the app's data there. The app's own Backup feature writes a file only to the place you choose.

Purchases

Google Play handles Premium payments. We do not see your card or payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to payments.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the last change.

Contact

Write to the developer email shown on the app's Google Play page, or open an issue at github.com/zeevy/grblcontroller/issues. See also the Terms of Service.
