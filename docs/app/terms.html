What the app does

The app controls CNC machines and lasers that run GRBL or grblHAL firmware. It connects to the machine over Bluetooth, USB or WiFi, and sends commands and G-code to it.

Safe use is your responsibility

  • A CNC machine or laser can damage the work, the machine or the tool, and can hurt people.
  • Check every G-code file and every setting before you run it.
  • Keep the machine's emergency stop within reach, and do not leave a running machine alone.
  • Wear the safety gear your machine needs, such as laser safety glasses.
  • A phone or its link can fail during a job. Do not depend on the app as your only way to stop the machine.
